llms.txt Viewer

Paste in one or more site URLs. For each one, this looks for an /llms.txt file at that site's root — per the llms.txt standard — and shows whatever it finds. Everything runs locally in your browser: there's no server component, which also means some sites will refuse the read even when their llms.txt exists, since the browser needs the site's permission to fetch it cross-origin — that shows up here as "Blocked by CORS," distinct from a site that's genuinely unreachable. Open the link directly in a new tab in that case.
