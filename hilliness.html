Hilliness Explorer

Measure regional terrain ruggedness from a place name or a hand-drawn bounding box.

Geocoding: OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Elevation: Open-Topo-Data (mapzen dataset), falling back to Open-Elevation if unreachable. Terrain Ruggedness Index follows Riley et al. (1999); Vector Ruggedness Measure here is a region-level simplification of Sappington et al. (2007), not the strict per-window raster version. Coarse sampling is a statistical approximation — most meaningful at city/county scale, not exhaustive terrain analysis. If the elevation service is unreachable, this tool shows an error instead of ever substituting invented data.
